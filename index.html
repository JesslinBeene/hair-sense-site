Questions

Is this a haircut or a massage?

Neither. Your hair isn’t cut, washed or styled, and it isn’t a clinical massage. It’s gentle brushing, light scalp scratching and hair play, purely to help you relax.

What do I do during a session?

Nothing. Sit back, get comfortable and let go. You can stay quiet, chat a little, or doze off. Jess will check in on pressure, and you can ask for changes any time.

Can children book?

Yes. Express Play is a great 15-minute reset for kids. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian present for the whole session.

I’ve never felt ASMR tingles. Will I still enjoy it?

Most people do. Even without tingles, slow, gentle touch is calming. If you’re not sure, Express Play is an easy way to try it.

How should I come prepared?

Come as you are, in something comfortable. Clean, dry hair is nice but not required.

Why do I have to wait for a confirmation?

Jess runs the studio herself and confirms each booking personally. Once she does, you’ll get the address and your reminders. If you need something sooner, call or text.
